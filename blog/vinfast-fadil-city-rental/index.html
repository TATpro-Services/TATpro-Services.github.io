If you’re looking for the most affordable and practical way to explore Ho Chi Minh City on your own terms, look no further than the VinFast Fadil. This compact hatchback is the most popular city car in Vietnam — and our most budget-friendly rental option.

Vietnam’s Home-Grown Champion

The VinFast Fadil is a source of national pride in Vietnam. Manufactured locally by VinFast (Vietnam’s first major domestic automaker), the Fadil represents a new era of Vietnamese automotive engineering. Beyond the patriotic appeal, though, this car simply makes sense for city driving.

Why the Fadil Wins in the City

Tiny Footprint, Big Convenience

Ho Chi Minh City’s streets can be narrow, and parking spots are fiercely contested. The Fadil’s compact dimensions (under 4 metres long) let you squeeze into spaces that larger cars can only dream of. Finding parking near Bến Thành Market, District 1 alleys, or local restaurants becomes genuinely stress-free.

Surprisingly Nippy

Don’t let the small size fool you — the Fadil’s 1.4L engine has enough punch to handle HCMC traffic comfortably. The smooth automatic gearbox makes stop-and-go city driving effortless.

Great Fuel Economy

With fuel prices fluctuating, efficiency matters. The Fadil returns excellent fuel consumption figures, meaning your rental costs stay low even if you’re covering lots of ground.
