Mitsubishi Attrage — Comfort & Efficiency for Long Trips

2026/08/05

TATpro Services

Car Reviews Road Trips

Not every trip stays within city limits. For those planning to venture beyond Ho Chi Minh City — whether it’s the cool highland air of Đà Lạt, the pristine beaches of Nha Trang, or the sand dunes of Mũi Né — the Mitsubishi Attrage is TATpro’s go-to recommendation for longer road trips.

A Sedan Built for Distance

The Attrage is a genuine full-size sedan with a 430-litre boot — one of the largest in its class. This translates to real-world practicality: suitcases, coolers, beach gear, and shopping bags all fit comfortably without anyone having to hold luggage on their lap.

What Sets the Attrage Apart

Outstanding Fuel Economy

On the highway between HCMC and Đà Lạt, the Attrage consistently returns exceptional fuel consumption. This directly reduces your travel costs compared to larger vehicles — a significant saving over a 300–400km trip.

Highway-Ready Comfort

Where smaller city cars can feel nervous at highway speeds, the Attrage’s longer wheelbase provides a stable, planted feeling. The cabin remains quiet even at 100+ km/h, keeping passengers refreshed on long journeys.

Professional Appearance

With its clean sedan lines and smart front fascia, the Attrage looks professional and presentable — perfect for business travel or making a good impression at your destination.
