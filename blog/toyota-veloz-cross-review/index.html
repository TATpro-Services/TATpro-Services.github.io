When it comes to family travel in Ho Chi Minh City, having the right vehicle makes all the difference. The Toyota Veloz Cross has quickly become one of our most requested rentals — and it’s easy to see why.

What Makes the Veloz Cross Special?

The Toyota Veloz Cross is a modern 7-seat MPV (Multi-Purpose Vehicle) that offers an outstanding combination of space, comfort, and practicality. Whether you’re exploring the city with friends, heading to the airport with the whole family, or planning a weekend road trip to Vũng Tàu or Đà Lạt, this car delivers.

Interior Space That Doesn’t Compromise

Unlike many 7-seat vehicles where the third row feels cramped, the Veloz Cross provides genuinely usable space in all three rows. Adults can sit comfortably in the back without feeling squeezed. The large boot means luggage won’t have to go on laps — a common complaint with smaller vehicles.

Smooth Automatic Transmission

Navigating HCMC traffic can be stressful enough without worrying about manual gear changes. The Veloz Cross’s smooth CVT automatic transmission makes city driving effortless, letting you focus on the road rather than the gearstick.
