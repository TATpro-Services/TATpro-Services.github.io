Toyota Veloz Cross — The Ideal Family MPV

The Toyota Veloz Cross is our most popular 7-seat vehicle, combining generous interior space with modern comfort features. Whether you need it for a family weekend getaway, a group tour around Ho Chi Minh City, or a long-distance road trip to Đà Lạt or Mũi Né, the Veloz Cross handles it all with ease.

Why Rent the Veloz Cross?

  • Spacious cabin: Three rows of seating comfortably fit 7 adults with luggage
  • Smooth automatic transmission: Easy to drive even in HCMC traffic
  • Modern safety features: ABS, dual airbags, reverse camera
  • Fuel efficient: Petrol engine with excellent city and highway economy
  • Reliable: Toyota’s legendary build quality and reliability
